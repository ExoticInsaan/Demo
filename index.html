Invite a friend, get a reward

One level only. You are rewarded when a friend you invited buys something, and nobody else in the chain earns from it.

Amit joins with Ravi’s link and buys a ₹500 energy pack. Ravi gets 10%, which is ₹50, paid from the sale after Amit’s payment is confirmed.
